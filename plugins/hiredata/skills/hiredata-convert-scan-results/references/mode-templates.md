# Output templates per mode

Every output ends with the standard record (see `standard-record.md`) and, when applicable, a `Caveats` line. Always show the conservative projection next to the expected one, and label both as projections.

## Open: get into a conversation

**Brief for the sender** (five lines)

1. The one number, in the customer's own terms.
2. Two supporting findings, one line each.
3. Projections: expected · conservative, from the scan.
4. The automation to start with and why.
5. The proposed next step and moment.

**Message to send**: in the customer's language, from the sender, under 120 words.

- Opens with the one number.
- One sentence on what it means: people or clients they already have, reachable now.
- One next step: a 20-minute walkthrough of their own scan, with a proposed moment. For prospects the step is always seeing the results together, never buying.
- No scores, no methodology, no vendor internals, at most three numbers.

**Log line**: customer · scan date · lead finding with number · projected outcome · recommended automation #1 · next step and date.

## Review: go through the results together

A 20 to 30 minute call guide the sender can run from.

1. **Opening (2 min).** What the scan is (a read-only look at their own ATS data) and is not (a judgement). Then the one number.
2. **Their database today (5 min).** Scope and health in plain words. Reachability and completeness as "how many people can we actually talk to". Dormant share as "people you already paid to find".
3. **Three opportunities (10 min).** For each: the finding, the use case in Starts / Does / Writes / You get form, the projection with the conservative figure first, and one question that tests whether it is real for them ("who owns these candidates today?", "what happens when a client goes quiet?").
4. **Where to start (5 min).** The first automation, what it needs from them (channel, template approval, audience decision), what week one looks like.
5. **Close (3 min).** Next step and owner. Prospects: the path to a pilot. Customers: the activation date.
6. **Likely objections, answered from the scan.** "Our data is too messy" (the scan shows which part, and the first automation cleans it). "Candidates will find it spammy" (one personal message, their consent, their reply updates their own record). "We tried email before" (show the reply-rate assumption behind the projection and the conservative case). "No time to set this up" (what the agent configures versus what they decide).

## Sell: proposal or business case

One page, in the customer's language.

1. **Starting point.** Scope, placements last 12 months, recruiters, data health, in one paragraph.
2. **What the scan found.** Three opportunities with counts.
3. **What HireData would run.** Three use cases in Starts / Does / Writes / You get form, in activation order (month 1, month 2, later).
4. **What it could mean.** Projected placements per year and hours per week, expected and conservative, with the one-line assumption behind each. Results so far when they are a customer.
5. **What it takes.** The channel to connect, one template to approve, one audience decision, the week-one plan.
6. **Plan.** The plan or edition the sender supplied; otherwise a description by task volume ("a plan covering at least N tasks per month for these three automations"). No prices or promotions unless supplied.
7. **Next step.** One ask with a date.

## Language

Match the customer's language. Dutch default tone: informal "je", direct, warm, no corporate filler, no em dashes. English: plain and friendly.
