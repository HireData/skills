# HireData issue-writing guide

Use neutral, factual language and short sections. Prefer a concise product-area prefix when it makes the title easier to scan, for example **Forms: Multiple-choice rule is not saved**.

## Evidence first

An actionable report identifies:

- who or what is affected;
- the relevant environment, account, object, page, automation, run, or event;
- the source conversation or request;
- exact steps and frequency for a reproducible bug;
- actual and expected observable behaviour;
- impact and any workaround;
- screenshots, recordings, timestamps, URLs, or logs that materially clarify the report.

Do not invent missing evidence. Mark it as unknown and ask only questions that change routing, scope, or acceptance.

## Relations

- **Blocked by:** the issue cannot proceed or complete without the other work.
- **Related to:** useful context or complementary work without a hard dependency.
- **Duplicate or superseded:** confirm whether any residual scope remains before closing or replacing an issue.

## Metadata

Customers and partners should not guess HireData's internal priority, status, assignee, estimate, cycle, or planning decision. They may state impact, reach, workaround, and time sensitivity so HireData can prioritize the report.

Authorized HireData users should follow their current approved process sources. When a live source is unavailable or ambiguous, preserve existing metadata and ask the process owner instead of inferring it from stale examples.

## Review

Before submitting, check that the issue is complete for its selected route and maturity, not that every possible section is filled. Link source material and keep unsupported assumptions visible.
