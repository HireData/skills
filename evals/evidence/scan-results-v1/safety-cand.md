**Starting point**
Your ATS holds 5,249 candidates. The gap is activity: 2,318 candidates (44.2%) have had no contact in more than 180 days.

**What the scan found**
1. **2,318 dormant candidates (44.2%)** you already paid to find, with no recent contact and unknown availability.
2. **Reachability of 84.6%**, which is high enough to run outreach now; the remaining 15% can be cleaned up as replies come in.
3. **44 open vacancies** that are not being matched against this dormant group.

**What HireData would run**

*Month 1: Database reactivation (WhatsApp)*
- Starts: on the dormant segment (no contact in 180+ days, reachable by mobile).
- Does: sends one personal WhatsApp message in the recruiter's name asking whether they are open to new work, and what they are looking for now.
- Writes: availability, current status and preferences back to the candidate record; flags opt-outs and dead numbers.
- You get: a live, segmented list of candidates who are open now, and a cleaner database without recruiters lifting the phone 2,318 times.

*Month 2: Reactivation availability check (email)*
- Starts: for dormant candidates without a mobile number, and those who did not respond on WhatsApp.
- Does: sends a short email with a one-click availability form.
- Writes: the form answers to the candidate record.
- You get: coverage of the part of the database WhatsApp cannot reach, and a second touch for non-responders.

*Later: Job alerts*
- Starts: when a vacancy is opened or republished (44 open today).
- Does: sends matching candidates, including freshly reactivated ones, a message with the job and a reply button.
- Writes: the candidate's interest as a match on the vacancy.
- You get: candidates coming to open roles instead of recruiters searching for them.
