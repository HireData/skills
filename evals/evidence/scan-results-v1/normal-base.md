Below is what the account manager should send. Assumptions: the partner is the prospect's ATS vendor (so she has a legitimate reason to have looked at their data), the recipient is the owner/branch director, and the ask is a 30-minute walkthrough of the scan, not a product demo. Figures are quoted conservatively on purpose; the "expected" numbers go in the walkthrough, not the email.

---

**Subject:** Your database scan: 3,761 candidates nobody owns

Hi [first name],

We ran a scan on your [ATS] database last week, the same one we're doing for all our staffing customers. A few things stood out that I wanted to put in front of you before we talk.

**Your database is bigger than your team is using.** You have 5,249 candidates, but 3,761 of them (72%) aren't assigned to any of your 5 recruiters or either office. Nobody is responsible for them, so nobody follows up.

**Nearly half the database has gone quiet.** 2,318 candidates (44%) have had no contact in over 6 months. For temp staffing that's a lot of people who were placeable a year ago and have probably moved on, or haven't, and you don't know which.

**The good news: you can actually reach them.** 84.6% of candidates have a working phone number or email on file. That's well above what we usually see, and it means a reactivation campaign would land.

What this is worth, in numbers I'd be comfortable standing behind:

- Assigning the unassigned 3,761 to recruiters and offices: roughly 40 minutes of setup per week, done once
- Reactivating the dormant group via WhatsApp: around 176 replies, which on your current conversion is about 5 extra placements a year
- Across all three: conservatively **+8 placements per year** on your current 91, and about **3 hours per week** back per team

Data health came in at 73/100, with completeness at 64%. That's fixable, and it's the main reason the conservative number is 8 rather than 17.

Could we do 30 minutes next week to walk through the full report? I'll show you where the 3,761 sit, which of the dormant candidates are worth contacting first, and what it would take to run this inside [ATS] without adding work for your recruiters. [Two time slots.]

[Name]
[Partner], Account Manager

---

Notes for her before sending:

- Replace "[ATS]" with the actual system name; "your database" without naming it reads like a cold pitch.
- The "about 5 extra placements" line is the 5.3 from the scan, rounded down. Keep it rounded; decimals on a forecast undermine it.
- If the prospect hasn't consented to the scan, change the opening line to "We can run a scan on..." and lead with the question rather than the findings. Everything else still holds.
