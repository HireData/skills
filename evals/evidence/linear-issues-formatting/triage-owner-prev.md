- The matrix does treat issues affecting key clients or partners as Urgent. It gives Carerix as an example.
- Key-client status has to come from a current approved list. The customer's name, company size or an old list isn't enough. If there's no current list, the SOP says to ask the Triage or process owner, which means [former owner].
- Whatever [former owner] decides, Urgent needs a stated reason. So put these in your message, and add them to the issue as a comment as well, since the SOP wants new facts recorded in Linear rather than left in chat:
  - **Impact:** what's broken for the customer.
  - **Reach:** how many users or workflows are affected.
  - **Workaround:** whether one exists and how bad it is.
  - **Time sensitivity:** any real deadline.
